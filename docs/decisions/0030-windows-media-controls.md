# 0030: Nova's music in Windows' media controls

19 November 2026 · Agreed with the user in chat ("yes"), after a throwaway test on his PC · Built

**Context.** Music Nova plays on the PC satellite (0026) was invisible to Windows: nothing in the volume pop-up, and the media keys, Numpad Enter's play/pause among them, did nothing to it. Windows' own media system, the System Media Transport Controls, fixes that, and HASS.Agent's Windows media player, which reads them, then shows Nova's music in Home Assistant too. A test on 19 November showed a windowless `pythonw` started by Task Scheduler can register as a media session through `winrt` and receives play, pause, next and previous at once - but setting the button flags in one chained line left next and previous off without an error, and Windows named the session "pythonw.exe".

**Decisions.**

1. **Said in the hello.** A satellite which can show its music in Windows' media controls says `media_controls: true` in its hello: only a push-to-talk satellite, on Windows, with `winrt` importable (`voice_assistant/windows_media.py`). Elsewhere `winrt` is never imported, and the hello is as it was.
2. **The brain says what plays.** For such a satellite, and only with a music player, its session reads its own player's `now_playing()` every 5 seconds (as for Home Assistant's player) and straight after a button, and sends `now_playing {"state", "track", "artist", "album"}` when any of those change (media_controls.py). Music elsewhere, Spotify's `here: False`, counts as stopped. The cover goes as the message's payload, with `"cover": true`, only when it's a new one; `"cover": false` takes the last away when the new song has none, or its cover can't be had or is too big for a message. Stopped, the satellite clears everything, the cover with it.
3. **Windows shows it.** The satellite shows the track, artist, album, cover and playing or paused as a music session, with play, pause, next and previous each switched on in a line of its own, and clears and switches it off when stopped, or when it loses the brain. Speech is never shown.
4. **The buttons act on this satellite's own music.** Each goes to the brain as `media {"action"}`. Pause pauses it and keeps it paused once Nova finishes talking; play carries on what's paused there, or with nothing loaded plays as Home Assistant's play button does; next and previous skip. Never the rest of the room's: the room-wide rules for what's said (0026) are unchanged. Then `now_playing` goes at once, even unchanged. Presses are logged, what plays isn't.
5. **Named Nova.** The satellite says it is `Nova.Satellite` (its AppUserModelID) before registering, and `install.ps1` makes a Start-menu shortcut "Nova" with that AppUserModelID, pointing at the launcher as the logon task does, so Windows says "Nova". A shortcut which can't be made is a warning, not a failed install.
6. **Packages.** The six `winrt` packages are pinned in `requirements-pc.txt` at the versions on the PC on 19 November.

**What it costs.** One more read of the player every 5 seconds for the PC, which for Spotify is another request to Spotify's servers. Opening the "Nova" shortcut from the Start menu starts a second launcher, whose satellite can't have the press port and so stops and is started again, every five minutes at most, until logoff. No seek bar, position or volume: Windows' slider stays the system volume. Nothing changes on the Pi.
