# 0024: The PC as a push-to-talk satellite

18 November 2026 · Phase 6, first piece · Agreed with the user in chat, and built · Design: [the spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-pc-push-to-talk-satellite-design.md)

**Context.** The user wants to talk to Nova at his Windows PC by pressing a key, and have the PC answer like a second Nova in the room: replies and music on its speakers, and everything the brain can do working from it. The Pi stays the always-on satellite in the same room (the bedroom). This is the first of four pieces, in his order: the PC as a push-to-talk satellite; opening apps, games and pages on the PC and PC commands said at it; fingerprint approval for big actions; the Mac. 0006 built the brain for several satellites, so the PC is a satellite, not a new client.

**Decision.** The Pi's satellite program runs on Windows with a push-to-talk mode, in four pieces.

1. **The satellite's mode.** `satellite.trigger` is `wake_word` (the default, so the Pi is unchanged) or `push_to_talk`. In push-to-talk the satellite loads no wake word, voice check or wake clips (`openwakeword` is imported only when the wake word is used, so the PC installs neither it nor scipy and scikit-learn), and says it is push-to-talk in its `hello`. A press reaches it on `127.0.0.1:<satellite.press_port>` (default 10401), where `voice_assistant/press.py` answers only `POST /press` with the header `X-Nova-Press: 1` and a `Host` of `127.0.0.1:<port>`, so no web page and nothing else on the network can make the PC listen. A press when idle plays `listening.wav` at once, from the satellite, whatever `sounds.listening_enabled` says, and records with no pre-roll; a press while recording ends it and sends what was said; a press over a reply stops it and listens, as the wake word does. Mute doesn't block a press. The microphone stream opens on a press and closes when the recording ends, and the speaker stream opens when something is to play and closes after `IDLE_CLOSE_SECONDS` (5) with nothing sounding, so the PC still sleeps after its 30 idle minutes and Windows' microphone light is on only while Nova listens. `LIMIT_SECONDS` and `MOST_SECONDS` still bound every recording.
2. **The brain's side.** `wake` carries `pressed: true` for a pressed recording, which the brain treats like a woken one with no pre-roll and without playing the listening sound again. The brain keeps each satellite's kind and last known Home Assistant area (`satellite_rooms.py`, in `satellite_rooms.json`), so both are known while it's disconnected or Home Assistant can't be reached. Pressed with nothing but noise heard, it says nothing.
3. **The room acts as one.** A timer or alarm set at a push-to-talk satellite is handed, as it is set, to an always-on satellite with the same area, connected or not, else the default satellite; it stays on the PC only if the PC is the only satellite. The reply says where it will ring only if that isn't the same room. "Stop", a press or a wake, "pause", "resume" and volume act on every satellite in the room, whichever was spoken to; where new music starts, where replies play and what was asked stay with the satellite spoken to. A satellite with no known area is a room of its own, so the Pi alone behaves as before.
4. **Running it on Windows, and the deploy.** `scripts/windows/launcher.pyw` runs the satellite with the venv's `pythonw.exe` (no window) and starts it again whenever it exits, after 2 s, doubling to at most 300 s while it keeps failing in under a minute, and back to 2 s after a run of a minute or more. A Task Scheduler task, `Nova satellite`, starts it at his logon, in his session, so it has his microphone and speakers. Everything is under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\nova` (`app`, `config`, `data`), his user's only. `scripts/windows/install.ps1` makes that, a venv from `py -3` (the `python` on the PC's PATH is the Microsoft Store stub), installs `requirements-pc.txt` (the six packages at `requirements.lock`'s versions, plus cffi and pycparser) only when it changed, writes `config\config.yaml` if there is none, and replaces the task. It never writes `.env`: the password from `add-satellite pc` goes in by hand. `scripts/deploy_pc.sh`, run on CT 109, reads the commit the brain runs (CT 107's checkout), refuses unless this repository has it, sends its `git archive` over `scp`, runs `install.ps1` over `ssh pc` and ends the running satellite, which the launcher starts again with the new code. The PC gets no key to anything, and never unreleased work, so the protocol always matches the brain's.

**The calls.**

- No "Hey Nova" on the PC. It shares a room with the Pi, so both would wake on every wake word.
- Pressing again while listening sends what he said, rather than throwing it away; two quick presses over a reply just silence it.
- The PC's settings file names only the brain's address (`nova:10400`), its name (`pc`, since its machine name is `nathanspc`), `satellite.trigger: push_to_talk` and `audio.echo_cancellation: false`. The echo canceller loads `libspeexdsp`, which the PC hasn't, and a press pauses the music and stops a reply first, so there is little to cancel; its alignment across the speaker stream's gaps is untested, so it stays off.
- Timers go to the Pi because the PC is often asleep or off, and an alarm which goes unheard is a failure.
- A logoff or shutdown is an unclean stop for the satellite (Windows never sends `SIGTERM`), and so is the deploy ending it. Accepted.
- Updating is from CT 109 over `ssh pc`, not from GitHub, and each run is his named job. `launcher.pyw` itself is replaced by a deploy, but the running launcher keeps the code it started with until the task is stopped and started again, or he logs on again.
- The key's script (`flow100.ahk`, homelab repo, `desktop/pc/`) must post to `http://127.0.0.1:10401/press` with `X-Nova-Press: 1`, not to `localhost`, whose `Host` the listener refuses.

**Not done here.** Opening apps and PC commands, fingerprint approval and the Mac. The echo canceller's Windows library, and Spotify Connect on the PC (Navidrome is what plays music now). The press goes by a local port, not a named pipe.

**Setting it up (his named jobs).** Merge `pc-satellite` and deploy the brain; `add-satellite pc` on the brain, its password into the PC's `%LOCALAPPDATA%\nova\config\.env` as `NOVA_BACKEND_TOKEN`; `scripts/deploy_pc.sh`; the Bedroom area for the `nova_nathanspc` device in Home Assistant; `flow100.ahk`'s Numpad 3 changed to send the press; then his checks on the PC: a question, music, volume, a light, a timer (which should ring on the Pi), pressing 3 to stop listening and to stop a reply, the PC going to sleep and coming back, and the brain being restarted.
